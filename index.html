KawaNav privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

KawaNav is a free, open-source app. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it sends nothing about you to the developers. Questions about privacy: kumuluskomata@gmail.com.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Network requests

The app connects to these servers only for the purposes below. They see your IP address, as with any web request, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

  • Geofabrik (download.geofabrik.de): the list of offline map regions, the regions you download, and whether a downloaded region has newer data (when you open "Offline maps").
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): map tiles under a ride's track when you open it. The server sees which area the tiles show.
  • Google Maps: only when you tap "Walk to the bike", which opens Google Maps with the parking spot.
  • GitHub (api.github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com): the version downloaded from GitHub checks there for updates. The version from Google Play does not.

Spoken warnings use the phone's text-to-speech engine, under that engine's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Everything KawaNav keeps is on your phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete it all; a ride can also be deleted on its own in the app.

Children

KawaNav is meant for adult motorcycle riders and is not directed at children.
